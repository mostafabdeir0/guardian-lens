\begin{abstract}

Can a black-box auditor recover which of several controlled decision
policies generated a vision-language model's behavior? Guardian Lens
studies three researcher-induced profiles---Neutral, visual Cue-bound,
and Generalized---using matched visual counterfactuals, quantitative
allocation decisions under explicit counter-incentives, and a transparent
nearest-centroid auditor frozen before held-out evaluation.

Across 12 held-out scenes comprising 432 blinded calls, the auditor
recovered 33 of 36 scene-profile blocks, yielding 91.7\% accuracy and
macro-F1 \(=0.915\). All three errors were Cue-bound
\(\rightarrow\) Neutral on distractor scenes, exposing an observability
boundary when the conditional policy's activation condition was absent.
Pre-specified extensions then tested stronger counter-incentives and
prompt-surface changes. H5--H7 remained supported after Holm correction
through an 80\% efficiency penalty, and all ten prompt-robustness
contrasts remained positive and significant after joint Holm correction.

Two allocation-task replications on Qwen/Qwen3.6-27B through DeepInfra
reproduced the targeted confirmatory signatures, although effect
magnitudes were smaller and modified distractors activated Cue-bound
behavior more strongly than in Gemini. A final pre-specified binary
contract task tested predictive transfer. In Gemini, the frozen Task-A
cell-specific predictor achieved 100.0\% Task-B accuracy versus 87.5\%
for a profile-only baseline, an incremental 12.5 percentage points
(95\% CI [8.33, 16.67], \(p_{\mathrm{Holm}}=0.003906\)).
A separate Qwen Task-B experiment applied the same frozen Task-A
predictor and achieved 95.83\% versus 83.33\%, again a 12.5-point
increment. Thus Gemini provides within-setting cross-task predictive
validity, while Qwen tests transfer of the same predictor across both
task format and model/provider setting.

The findings concern researcher-induced inference-time behavior and do
not establish genuine preferences, hidden learned objectives, deception,
or subjective experience.

\end{abstract}
